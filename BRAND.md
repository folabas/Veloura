# Veloura — Brand Brief

**Tagline:** For the Woman You Are.

Veloura is a contemporary modest fashion brand for the woman who wants to express herself through style while staying true to who she is. It brings together modesty, femininity and contemporary fashion, so women can dress with confidence and show their individuality.

**Range:** scarves, everyday clothing, abaya sets and statement pieces.

**Belief:** modest fashion can be versatile, beautiful and accessible without giving up style. Every woman deserves to feel good in what she wears, whether it's an ordinary day, a special occasion, or a day she wants to show a different side of herself.

## What Veloura represents

1. **Modesty with individuality.** Modesty doesn't mean giving up personal style. A woman can dress modestly and still express her personality, preferences and creativity.
2. **Effortless femininity.** A soft, feminine look that isn't limited to one style. Pieces can be understated, playful, elegant or expressive, depending on who wears them.
3. **Accessible luxury.** Inspired by the elegance and simplicity of high-end fashion, at accessible prices. Luxury here isn't only about price. It's about how a piece looks, how it makes her feel, and the care put into how it's presented.
4. **Self-expression.** Women aren't one-dimensional. Their style changes with mood, lifestyle and experience, and Veloura makes room for that.

## Who she is

She's a modern woman who values modesty, loves beautiful clothing and enjoys expressing herself through fashion. One day she wants a simple neutral outfit, the next a bold print. She might be looking for an everyday scarf, an elegant abaya set, or the piece that finishes an outfit.

She doesn't have to fit one aesthetic to belong here. Veloura meets her where she is.

## The experience

Shopping should feel **personal, welcoming and inspiring**. Every interaction should remind her that style is personal and that she can trust her own choices.

**Visual identity:** understated elegance, warm neutrals, refined typography, and the pieces themselves at the centre.

**Ambition:** to be more than a shop. Veloura should be a fashion brand women come back to for inspiration, self-expression, and pieces that fit naturally into their lives.

## "For the Woman You Are"

This is the heart of the brand. You don't have to become someone else to feel beautiful in what you wear. You can be soft or bold, understated or expressive, traditional or contemporary, and you can change and experiment over time.

Veloura doesn't tell a woman who to be. It gives her pieces that let her express who she already is. The website should get this across from the first moment someone lands on it.

---

## What this means for the website

- **Lead with the tagline and the clothes.** The hero should pair "For the Woman You Are" with strong product or editorial imagery. Keep the copy short.
- **Show range, not one look.** Homepage and collection pages should place soft neutrals next to bold prints, and everyday pieces next to occasion wear. The variety *is* the message.
- **Palette:** warm neutrals such as cream, sand, taupe and a deep cocoa brown (🤎) as the accent. Avoid stark black and white and loud sale colours.
- **Typography:** a refined serif for headings with a clean sans-serif for body and UI. Use generous spacing and leave plenty of white space.
- **Accessible luxury in the details:** good photography, careful product descriptions, considered packaging and presentation shots, and a calm checkout. Avoid discount-heavy banners that make the brand feel cheap.
- **Tone of voice:** warm, inclusive and second person ("you"). Never tell her what she should wear. Invite her to explore instead.
- **Browsing by mood or occasion** (e.g. Everyday, Occasion, Statement), alongside browsing by category (Scarves, Clothing, Abaya Sets), supports the self-expression idea.
- **Room to grow into inspiration:** a lookbook or styling section, even a small one at first, matches the goal of being more than a store.

## Design reference

**Primary:** Rino & Pelle (https://www.landing.love/sites/rino-pelle/). The live rino-pelle.com has since become a generic store (checked October 2026), so work from the landing.love recording and screenshots, not the live site. It uses an editorial serif mixed with italic, a warm off-white background, a hero made from a collage of portrait photos, a full-screen lifestyle collection banner, and a quiet product grid with tall cards and a category list on the left.

**Borrow from Special Production** (https://www.landing.love/sites/specialproduction/): a lookbook that scrolls sideways through full-height images and videos.

**Borrow from Floema Jewelry** (https://www.landing.love/sites/floema-jewelry/): a fan of tilted portrait cards above a single soft-serif line, on a full section of muted colour (dusty rose, lilac). Use it for the "For the Woman You Are" section, with a different woman or look on each card.

**Borrow from Laura Monin** (https://www.landing.love/sites/lauramonin/): one large serif word over a loose scatter of photos. Use it to introduce a print collection.

**Not Zes Nullen.** Its circle crops would cut off the scarf drape, and its bold sans-serif type and lime accent clash with the warm neutral palette.
